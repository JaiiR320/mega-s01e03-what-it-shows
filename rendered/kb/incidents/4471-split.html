Closing #4471 does not cover the one-day moves. The parser fix changes how day and month are read, so it does not explain these reports. Do not tell these reporters it is fixed.

Hint reports got no code fix: the form that printed the hint is gone. Check that alder.physio, copper.studio and pale.harbour have moved off it, because the tenant directory in this export still lists them on booking-v3.

How they were told apart

  • Parser: a day-first tenant’s date comes out with day and month swapped, on every way in except the one below.
  • Hint: the same swap, through the form of a tenant on booking-v3. The hint tells people to type the month first. The tenant and channel decide this, so it is not visible in the report text.
  • One day: the date moves a day, or differs between two readers. A swap cannot do this, so these reports leave parser, where the desk filed them by tenant. Time zones are the likely cause, but the export cannot prove it.

What not to trust

  • Subject lines. Both swap bugs use the same subjects, which is how decision 0001 merged them. Changing that rule takes a new decision record.
  • Intake tags. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant.
  • The raw export. Its pages overlap, so count by report id.
